## What is StarBase 118?

**StarBase 118** is one of the oldest continuously-running **_Star Trek_ play-by-email role-playing games** on the internet. Since 1994, our members have written collaborative fiction together — a novel written by a crew, one post at a time — across a fleet of starships and starbases set in the _Star Trek_ universe.

It's like writing a novel, with friends. There's no winning or losing — just the story, and the people you write it with.

## Why our members stay

- ✍️ **Improve your writing** — get better with every post, with feedback from people who care about the craft
- 🤝 **Built on collaboration** — no competition, just a shared story everyone is invested in
- 🌍 **An international community** — write with players across many countries and time zones
- 🛰️ **Always something on** — missions, contests, taskforces, and fleet-wide events
- 🖖 **More than a game** — a crew, a club, and a creative home. Over **70% of members stay a year or more.**

## Open source you can use

We build a lot of our community's tooling in the open. A couple of pieces other communities can lift and reuse:

### 🏳️‍🌈 [star-trek-lgbtq-emoji](https://github.com/StarBase118/star-trek-lgbtq-emoji)
19 _Star Trek_-themed LGBTQ pride emoji for Discord, Slack, and forums — drop-in 128px PNGs, free for any community to use. `CC BY-SA 4.0`

### 🔒 [sb118-private-sites](https://github.com/StarBase118/sb118-private-sites)
A WordPress must-use plugin that makes private multisite sub-sites _actually_ private — closing the REST API and RSS side doors most "private site" plugins leave open. `PHP · GPL-2.0`
